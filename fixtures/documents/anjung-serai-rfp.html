Request for proposal: unified sales reporting

Anjung Serai Living runs 42 furniture stores in Malaysia and an online shop. We invite proposals for one view of store and online sales.

Contact: Farah Hanim, Head of Operations.

3. Requirements

3.1 Daily sales reporting for all 42 stores and the online shop, ready before stores open.

3.2 All data must stay in Malaysia or Singapore.

3.3 Training for store managers so they can use the reports themselves.

3.4 Move the online shop off our own servers by the end of Q2 2027.

4. Budget and timeline

The budget for the first phase is up to RM 150,000.

Submissions close on 30 November 2026. Shortlisted vendors present in the week of 7 December 2026.
